Accelerator

Program overview

A 6-month program for execution.

6-month structure: 3-month Structured Program + 3-month APAC Sandbox.
Small, focused cohorts of up to 10 companies.
US$150K via SAFE plus GPU and compute resources.
Months 1–3

Structured Program

1-on-1 mentoring by experienced founders and operators.
Corporate structuring support, research direction, and product strategy.
Startup execution and scaling guidance.
Months 4–6

APAC Sandbox

Live customer validation across 6 markets: HK, TW, SG, JP, TH, Cambodia.
Real revenue signals through live sales, partnerships, or LOIs.
Accelerated traction-building ahead of fundraising.

Demo Day

Present live metrics. Raise on evidence.

Founders present live metrics to investors and community. Structured investor access and community-driven momentum.
